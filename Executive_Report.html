1   Objective

Analyze 12 months of e-commerce transaction data to quantify revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior and value drivers, product contribution, geographic concentration, cohort retention, and RFM-based segmentation — translating raw transactions into prioritized, actionable business recommendations.

5   Cohort Retention Analysis

Retention Patterns

MetricValue
Avg. Month-1 retention (2010 cohorts)~20.5%
Best Month-1 retentionDec 2009 cohort: 35.1%
Peak single-period retentionDec 2009, Month 11: 49.6%
Worst Month-1 retentionNov 2010 cohort: 10.7%

Key observations:

  • Approximately 80% of newly acquired customers do not return in the following month, highlighting customer retention as the largest growth opportunity.
  • Dec 2009 cohort shows non-linear re-engagement (retention increases at Months 3, 10, 11) — likely driven by seasonal/holiday triggers.
  • Later cohorts (Oct–Nov 2010) show steep drop-offs partly due to truncated observation windows.
Opportunity: Even a 5 percentage-point improvement in Month-1 retention across all cohorts would retain hundreds of additional customers over time and translate to significant incremental revenue.
